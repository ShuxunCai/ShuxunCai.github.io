Welcome!

I am Shuxun Cai, a Ph.D. candidate in Finance at the School of Economics, Xiamen University of China, expecting to graduate in June 2028. I earned my Bachelor’s degree in Finance from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 2020.

My research interests encompass corporate payouts, the cost of debt financing, corporate carbon emissions, climate change, and climate innovation.

My research has been published in journals such as Financial Management, Public Finance Research (财政研究), and Pacific-Basin Finance Journal. I have received the 2025 National Scholarship for Doctoral Students, the Anta Annual Outstanding Student Scholarship, and the 2025–2026 Xiamen University Outstanding Merit Student Award, among others. I have also served as an anonymous reviewer for journals such as Journal of International Financial Management & Accounting, Humanities and Social Sciences Communications, and International Journal of Educational Development.

You can find my CV (Chinese; English) and bio (Chinese; English) here. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in my research!
