  1. Inalienable Human Capital and Inevitable Corporate Payouts （与 Kose John、Xiaoran Ni 和 Chi Zhang 合作）(joint with Kose John, Xiaoran Ni, and Chi Zhang)

    发表于 Financial Management，2025Published online in Financial Management, 2025 [Publication] [PDF]

    SummarySummary

    我们强调，鉴于企业支付，人力资本的不可让渡性质可能对股东与其他利益相关方之间的经济收益分配产生关键影响。本文利用美国15个州先后否决“不可避免披露原则”（IDD）所形成的外生冲击；这些冲击可能提高关键人才的流动性与议价能力。研究发现，在这些事件发生后，相较于控制组企业，处理组企业提高了支付水平。基准效应在更依赖关键人力资本、公司治理更完善、财务状况更稳健以及面临更激烈产品市场竞争的企业中更为显著。这些发现表明，提高支付水平使股东能够阻止以离职相威胁的关键人才攫取经济租金。也就是说，由于股东采取反制行动，作为劳动者子群体的关键人力资本所具有的不可让渡性质，可能在无意中导致经济收益分配对劳动者整体更加不利。

    We highlight that the inalienable nature of human capital can crucially determine the division of economic gains between shareholders and other counterparts in view of corporate payouts. Exploiting the staggered rejections of the inevitable disclosure doctrine (IDD) across 15 US states as exogenous shocks that potentially increase the mobility and bargaining power of key talents, we find that treatment firms increase payouts relative to control firms following those events. The baseline effects are more pronounced among firms that are more reliant on key human capital, have better corporate governance and stronger financial conditions, and are faced with greater product market competition. These findings suggest that higher payouts enable shareholders to deter the capture of economic rents by key talents who threaten to leave. That is, due to shareholders’ counteraction, the inalienable nature of key human capital, a subgroup of labor, may unintendedly result in an unfavorable division of economic gains for labor as a whole.

  2. Administrative Monopoly Regulation and the Cost of Debt Financing: Evidence from China （与 Xiaoran Ni 合作）(joint with Xiaoran Ni)

    发表于 Pacific-Basin Finance Journal，2025Published in Pacific-Basin Finance Journal, 2025 [Publication] [PDF]

    Summary

    传统反垄断研究主要关注基于市场形成的垄断，而对由行政权力形成的垄断关注较少。本文考察旨在遏制行政垄断的中国公平竞争审查制度（FCRS）如何影响企业债务融资成本。研究发现，行政垄断规制降低了债务融资成本，提高会计信息质量和降低企业违约风险是两个可能的作用渠道。主要效应在民营企业、中小企业以及金融市场化程度较低地区的企业中更加显著。公平竞争审查制度还有助于解决“僵尸企业”问题，减轻其对正常企业债务融资的挤出效应。此外，当审查措施同时包括严格的自我审查和外部监督时，债务融资成本下降得更多。这些发现凸显了行政垄断规制的重要性，并有助于推动亚太地区的反垄断实践。

    Traditional antitrust research has primarily focused on the market-based monopoly, while less attention given to those formed by administrative power. This study explores how the Fair Competition Review System (FCRS) in China, designed to curb administrative monopoly, influences corporate debt financing costs. We find that administrative monopoly regulation reduces the cost of debt financing. Enhanced accounting information quality and lower corporate default risk are two possible channels. The main effect is more pronounced among private firms, small-sized and medium-sized firms, and firms in regions with lower degree of financial marketization. The FCRS also aids in addressing the issue of “zombie firms”, mitigating their crowding-out effect on the debt financing of normal firms. Moreover, the cost of debt financing decreases more when the review measures include both stringent self-inspection and external supervision. These findings underscore the importance of administrative monopoly regulation and contribute to the advancement of antitrust endeavors in the Asia-Pacific region.

  3. How Education Reduces Urban Relative Poverty? Evidence from China （与 Kun Zhang 和 Yuanyuan Wang 合作）(joint with Kun Zhang and Yuanyuan Wang)

    发表于 Journal of the Asia Pacific Economy，2023Published in Journal of the Asia Pacific Economy, 2023 [Publication] [PDF]

    Summary

    既有研究主要关注中国的绝对贫困问题。然而，2018年中国城市居民的相对贫困发生率已达到23.90%。本文研究通过教育减少城市相对贫困的作用机制。研究发现，家庭受教育年限的增加能够通过改善社会资本这一间接途径帮助城市家庭摆脱相对贫困。进一步研究发现，儒家文化影响越大、市场化程度越低，社会资本在教育与减贫之间的中介作用越强。

    Previous studies mainly focus on absolute poverty in China. However, the rate of relative poverty of Chinese urban residents has reached 23.90% in 2018. This paper investigates the mechanism for reducing urban relative poverty through education. Findings are that the increase of family education years can help urban households get rid of relative poverty through the indirect way of improving social capital. Further study finds that greater influence of Confucian culture and weaker marketization degree will result in stronger mediating role of social capital between education and poverty reduction.

  1. 低碳供应链视角下的留抵退税政策效应研究（与倪骁然、李润和金奇合作）The Policy Effects of VAT Credit Refunds from a Low-carbon Supply-chain Perspective (joint with Xiaoran Ni, Run Li, and Qi Jin)

    发表于《财政研究》，2026Published in Public Finance Research, 2026 (in Chinese) [Publication] [PDF]

    Summary

    推动供应链绿色化低碳化发展，是建设现代化产业体系、推进经济社会发展全面绿色转型的内在要求。文章以增值税留抵退税为研究对象，基于供应链绿色低碳转型的视角，使用较为精细的上市企业及其上下游碳排放数据，系统考察“双碳”背景下财税改革的政策效应。研究表明，增值税留抵退税政策会使得企业的供应链碳排放强度显著下降，表明其促进了企业对供应链进行绿色低碳管理。上述效应在高碳、非技术密集型行业，以及受到较强外部监督和较大客户影响的企业中更为显著。机制分析显示，增值税留抵退税的供应链降碳途径主要体现在两个方面，一是降低供应商集中度，引入更低碳环保的供应商；二是提高对供应商的话语权，推动其提高绿色创新水平。整体而言，作为一类与供应链上下游密切相关的财税政策，增值税留抵退税在支持企业打造低碳供应链上具有独特优势，不过仍需加强与其他政策的统筹协调。文章的发现为“十五五”时期通过财税支持政策促进产业链供应链绿色化、推动落实碳排放双控制度提供了有益参考。

    Promoting the greening and low-carbon development of supply chains constitutes an intrinsic requirement for fully and accurately implementing the new development philosophy and advancing the comprehensive green transformation of economic and social development. This paper examines the value-added tax (VAT) credit refund policy within the framework of low-carbon supply chain transformation. Utilizing granular carbon emission data from listed companies and their upstream and downstream partners, this study systematically investigates the policy effects of fiscal and tax reforms in the context of the “Dual Carbon” goals. The findings indicate that the VAT credit refund policy significantly reduces carbon emission intensity within corporate supply chains, demonstrating its role in incentivizing enterprises to enhance green and low-carbon management across their supply networks. The above effects are more pronounced in high-carbon industries, non-technology-intensive industries, and firms subject to stronger external oversight and greater customer influence. Mechanism analysis reveals that the policy’s supply chain carbon reduction effects encompass two main channels: first, reducing supplier concentration and introducing suppliers with lower carbon emissions and greater environmental sustainability; second, increasing bargaining power over suppliers to promote their green innovation. Overall, as a fiscal and tax tool closely tied to upstream and downstream supply chain operations, the VAT credit refund mechanism possesses distinct advantages in supporting firms to build low-carbon supply chains. However, its effectiveness still requires strengthened coordination with other policy measures. The findings of this paper provide useful references for promoting the green transformation of industrial and supply chains through fiscal and tax support policies and advancing the implementation of the dual carbon control system during the 15th Five-Year Plan period.

  2. 碳排放权交易、能源利用效率与经济增长——基于异时DID方法的讨论（与张琨合作）Carbon Emissions Trading, Energy-use Efficiency, and Economic Growth: Evidence from a Staggered DID Design (joint with Kun Zhang)

    发表于《城市发展研究》，2022Published in Urban Development Studies, 2022 (in Chinese) [Publication] [PDF]

    Summary

    2021年7月，我国统一的全国碳市场正式启动。作为世界上最大的碳排放权交易市场，中国碳市场究竟能否兼顾减排目标和经济增长目标？八年碳试点为全国碳市场的建立奠定了制度基础，研究碳试点对经济增长的影响机制有助于预测全国碳市场对经济增长的影响。考虑到各省开展碳排放权交易试点的时间不同，采用更符合实际的异时双重差分方法（异时DID），发现碳排放权交易试点显著促进了经济增长。通过中介效应分析发现，碳排放权交易试点对经济增长的促进作用是通过提高能源利用效率来实现的。进一步对东西部省份进行了子样本分析，回归结果表明，在东部省份，碳试点通过能源利用效率促进经济增长的中介效应要更加显著。最后，为如何在实现碳减排目标的同时促进经济稳定增长，提出了政策建议。

    In July 2021, China’s unified national carbon market was officially launched. As the world’s largest carbon emission trading market, can China’s carbon market achieve both emission reduction targets and economic growth targets? The ten-year carbon pilot has laid an institutional foundation for the establishment of the national carbon market. Studying the impact mechanism of the carbon pilot on economic growth is helpful to predict the impact of the national carbon market on economic growth. Considering the different time of carrying out carbon emission trading pilot in different provinces, this paper adopts the more practical heterogeneous timing DID method (Heterogeneous Timing DID), and finds that the carbon emission trading pilot has significantly promoted economic growth. Through the intermediary effect analysis, it is found that the promotion of carbon emission trading pilot to economic growth is realized by improving energy efficiency. The sub samples containing only eastern provinces are further analyzed. The regression results show that in the eastern provinces, the intermediary effect of carbon pilot to promote economic growth through energy efficiency is more significant. Finally, this paper puts forward policy suggestions on how to achieve the goal of carbon emission reduction and promote stable economic growth.

  3. 金融泡沫形成与防范——理论机制与基于实验的经验证据（与王媛媛和张琨合作）The Formation and Prevention of Financial Bubbles: Theoretical Mechanisms and Experimental Evidence (joint with Yuanyuan Wang and Kun Zhang)

    发表于《投资研究》（封面文章），2022Published in Review of Investment Studies (Cover Article), 2022 (in Chinese) [Publication] [PDF]

    Summary

    防控金融资产泡沫是防范系统性金融风险的重中之重。实验金融学对金融泡沫的研究弥补了传统经济学研究的不足，拓展了对金融泡沫的认知边界。从金融泡沫产生的原因来看，实验金融学文献为有限理性假说提供了充足的证据，这些文献发现交易者本身并非是理性的，他们对金融资产价格的内在基本价值估计存在偏差或者混淆、或是具有先验的异质信念、抑或受到情绪的影响，从而导致金融泡沫的产生。从预防和抑制金融泡沫的制度设计和相关政策研究方面来看，实验金融学的研究成果发现熔断机制、涨跌停限制、卖空制度等都无法有效地抑制金融泡沫，而金融衍生品、合理的市场交易制度等可以有效减少金融泡沫。这些具有洞见性的实验研究证据，为我们下一步的学术研究与政策制定提供了良好的理论与方法基础。

    Preventing and controlling asset bubbles is the priority in preventing systemic financial risks. The study of financial asset bubbles by experimental economics has made up for the deficiency of traditional economics and expanded the existing research on asset bubbles. From the point of view of the causes of financial bubbles, experimental financial literature provides sufficient evidence for the bounded rationality hypothesis, which finds that traders themselves are not rational. They estimate bias or confusion in the estimation of intrinsic value of financial assets, or have a priori heterogeneous beliefs, and influenced by emotions, which leads to the emergence of financial bubbles. From the point of view of institutional design and related policy research on preventing and restraining the financial bubble, the results of experimental finance find that circuit breaker mechanism, price limit and short selling system can not effectively suppress the financial bubble, while financial derivatives and reasonable market trading system can effectively reduce the financial bubble. The insights of experimental economics on financial asset bubbles provide a good theoretical and methodology foundation for further academic research, mechanism design and policy implication.
